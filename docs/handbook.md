## 1. Laboratory Access

### 1.1 General Access Requirements

All researchers must complete mandatory laboratory safety
training before receiving access to laboratory facilities.

To request access:

1. Complete the laboratory safety training.
2. Submit an access request through the internal portal.
3. Obtain approval from the laboratory supervisor.
4. Activate your employee access card.
5. Follow all laboratory entry procedures.

Access requests are normally processed within three
working days.

### 1.2 Visitors

Visitors must register at reception and be accompanied
by an authorized employee.

Visitors are not permitted to operate laboratory equipment
without explicit authorization.

---

## 2. Laboratory Safety

### 2.1 Personal Protective Equipment

Researchers must wear the required protective equipment
when working in designated laboratory areas.

Depending on the activity, this may include:

- Laboratory coat
- Safety glasses
- Protective gloves
- Closed-toe shoes
- Additional equipment required by the experiment

### 2.2 Emergency Procedures

If an emergency occurs:

1. Stop the experiment immediately if safe to do so.
2. Move away from the hazardous area.
3. Activate the emergency alarm when necessary.
4. Contact the laboratory safety officer.
5. Follow evacuation instructions.

Do not attempt to repair malfunctioning equipment
during an emergency.

## 3. Equipment Management

### 3.1 Equipment Reservations

Researchers must reserve shared laboratory equipment
before use.

Reservations are made through the Equipment Booking Portal.

Each reservation must include:

- Researcher name
- Equipment identifier
- Date and expected duration
- Experiment description

### 3.2 Equipment Malfunctions

If laboratory equipment stops functioning:

1. Stop operating the equipment.
2. Place the equipment in a safe state.
3. Record any visible error messages.
4. Notify the equipment supervisor.
5. Submit a maintenance ticket.
6. Do not restart the equipment until authorized.

The equipment supervisor determines whether the
equipment can safely return to service.

### 3.3 Preventive Maintenance

Shared equipment undergoes scheduled preventive
maintenance once per month.

Maintenance records must include:

- Equipment identifier
- Maintenance date
- Work performed
- Responsible technician
- Observed issues

## 4. IT Support

### 4.1 Account Access

New employees receive an institutional account
after onboarding approval.

For account problems:

1. Open the IT Service Portal.
2. Select "Account and Access".
3. Describe the problem.
4. Submit a support ticket.

### 4.2 Password Problems

If a researcher forgets their password, they must
use the approved password reset system.

Passwords must never be shared with colleagues.

### 4.3 Software Installation

Installing software on managed laboratory computers
requires approval from the IT department.

Unauthorized installations are prohibited.

---

## 5. Data Management

### 5.1 Research Data Storage

Research data must be saved in approved institutional
storage systems.

Researchers must not store confidential research
data in personal cloud accounts.

### 5.2 Backups

Research data stored on approved project drives
is backed up daily.

Researchers are responsible for verifying that
critical experiment files are saved correctly.

### 5.3 Data Sharing

Confidential project information may only be shared
with authorized project members.

External data sharing requires approval from
the project supervisor.

## 6. Experiment Procedures

### 6.1 Starting an Experiment

Before starting an experiment:

1. Confirm that laboratory access is valid.
2. Verify equipment availability.
3. Review the experiment procedure.
4. Check equipment calibration status.
5. Wear the required protective equipment.
6. Record the experiment start time.

### 6.2 Experiment Failure

If an experiment fails:

1. Stop the experiment safely.
2. Record the observed failure.
3. Save relevant logs and measurements.
4. Notify the project supervisor.
5. Review possible causes.
6. Document the corrective action.

Repeated failures must be escalated to the
research team lead.

### 6.3 Experiment Documentation

Every experiment must have a digital record containing:

- Experiment identifier
- Researcher name
- Date
- Equipment used
- Experimental parameters
- Results
- Observed issues

## 11. Specialized Equipment Authorization

### 11.1 Authorization Requirements

Researchers may operate specialized laboratory equipment independently only after completing all required authorization steps.

These requirements include:

1. Completing general laboratory safety training.
2. Completing equipment-specific training.
3. Receiving approval from the equipment supervisor.
4. Confirming that their laboratory access card is active.
5. Verifying that the equipment is available and not under maintenance.

Completing general laboratory safety training does not automatically grant authorization to operate specialized equipment.

Equipment-specific authorization must be recorded in the laboratory training system.

### 11.2 Temporary Authorization

Researchers who have not completed equipment-specific training may observe specialized equipment demonstrations under the direct supervision of an authorized employee.

Observation does not grant permission to independently operate equipment.

Visitors may not independently operate equipment, even when accompanied by an authorized employee. Explicit authorization is required for any permitted visitor operation.

### 11.3 Authorization Suspension

Equipment authorization may be temporarily suspended following a safety incident, repeated procedure violations, or an expired training certification.

A researcher with suspended authorization must not independently operate the affected equipment until the equipment supervisor confirms that authorization has been restored.

## 12. Research Data Handling and Retention

### 12.1 Data Classification

Research data is categorized into three classifications:

- Public: Information approved for external distribution.
- Internal: Information intended for authorized institutional employees.
- Confidential: Sensitive research information restricted to authorized project members.

Confidential research data must be stored in approved institutional storage systems.

Personal cloud storage services are not permitted for confidential research data.

### 12.2 External Collaboration

Researchers who wish to share confidential project data with an external collaborator must obtain approval from the project supervisor before sharing.

The collaborator must also have the necessary authorization to access the information.

Project supervisor approval alone does not remove the requirement for appropriate recipient authorization.

### 12.3 Data Retention

Experiment records must be retained for at least five years after project completion.

Temporary intermediate files may be deleted when they are no longer required, provided that the final experiment record remains complete.

Critical experiment measurements and logs must be preserved when investigating a failed experiment.

### 12.4 Data Recovery

If a researcher accidentally deletes an important project file, they must contact IT support through the IT Service Portal.

Approved project drives are backed up daily.

Backup availability does not guarantee that every recent modification can be recovered.

## 13. Complex Operational Scenarios

### 13.1 Equipment Failure During an Experiment

If specialized equipment malfunctions during an active experiment, the researcher must:

1. Stop the experiment immediately if safe.
2. Place the equipment in a safe state.
3. Record visible error messages.
4. Preserve relevant experiment logs and measurements.
5. Notify the equipment supervisor.
6. Notify the project supervisor about the experiment failure.
7. Submit a maintenance ticket.
8. Document the incident in the experiment record.

The researcher must not restart the equipment until the equipment supervisor authorizes its return to service.

The experiment may only resume after equipment availability, calibration status, and required safety conditions have been verified.

### 13.2 New Researcher Starting an Experiment

A newly hired researcher who wishes to conduct an experiment independently must first:

1. Complete institutional onboarding.
2. Activate their institutional account.
3. Complete mandatory laboratory safety training.
4. Receive laboratory access approval.
5. Complete equipment-specific training when specialized tools are involved.
6. Obtain the necessary equipment authorization.
7. Reserve shared equipment through the Equipment Booking Portal.
8. Review the experiment procedure.
9. Verify the equipment's calibration and maintenance status.
10. Record the experiment start time.
